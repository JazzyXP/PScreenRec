# Mac companion third-party components

The companion includes CPython3.13.1, FFmpeg/FFprobe and relocated runtime libraries. Their license notices and runtime inventory are included under runtime/licenses and runtime/manifest.json. Consult that exact inventory and FFmpeg -buildconf for the bundled build; do not reuse the Windows Gyan FFmpeg version/checksum for Mac. FFmpeg runs as a separate executable.

Python: https://www.python.org/ . FFmpeg source and licensing: https://ffmpeg.org/ . Adobe Photoshop/Creative Cloud are separate products and not included.
