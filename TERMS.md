# Using PScreenRec

Last updated: 4 November 2026

PScreenRec is provided by JazzyXP (Den Mitin). Support: denp90@gmail.com.

You may install and use the published plugin and its companion application free of charge. All recording and export features are available without payment. Optional support of the developer does not purchase additional functionality.

You keep your rights to your drawings, recorded frames and exported videos. PScreenRec does not claim ownership of that content. Record only content you are entitled to capture and share.

Keep backups of important drawings and recordings. A timelapse records canvas states and is not a substitute for saving or backing up a Photoshop document. Rapid changes can be combined into one frame, and performance depends on canvas size, quality, computer resources and Photoshop version.

The currently supported release is for Windows x64. The separate PScreenRec Helper application is required for local storage and MP4 encoding. Internet access is needed for the initial FFmpeg download. No macOS support is promised by this release.

Third-party components retain their own licenses, including Python and FFmpeg. Adobe Photoshop is a separate product and requires its own appropriate license. PScreenRec is an independent project and is not an Adobe product or an endorsement by Adobe.

For questions, bug reports or clarification about distribution, contact denp90@gmail.com or use the project's GitHub issues page.
